Changes: Removed the dice-roll indices and the four hyphenated entries
(drop-down, felt-tip, t-shirt, and yo-yo), leaving 7,772 distinct words so
that a hyphen can unambiguously separate generated words. Word selection is
performed with replacement using cryptographic random numbers, not dice.
